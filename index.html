Fatigue Mechanics

High Impact

Player energy levels directly affect composite rating effectiveness through a fatigue multiplier.

Formula:
fatigue(energy, injured) { if (injured) return 0; energy += 0.05; if (energy > 1) energy = 1; return energy; } effectiveRating = compositeRating × fatigue(energy, injured)

Energy decreases by 0.08 × (1 - endurance) per play

Bench time recovers energy at 0.5 per minute

Injured players have 0% effectiveness

Positions with fatigue tracking: RB, WR, TE, DL, LB, CB, S

Catch Probability Calculation

Critical Impact

Multiple factors combine to determine if a pass is completed:

Formula:
baseProb = (qbAccuracy + receiverCatching) / 2 coverage = defenderCoverage × fatigue difficulty = passDepth × defenderProximity finalProb = baseProb × (1 - coverage) × (1 - difficulty)

QB accuracy composite vs receiver catching composite

Defender pass coverage reduces completion chance

Deep passes have lower completion probability

Tight coverage significantly reduces success

High Impact

Poor QB accuracy increases turnovers

Key Insights

• Multiplicative Effects: Most formulas multiply offense vs defense ratings, making balanced teams stronger than unbalanced ones

• Fatigue Impact: Player energy levels directly multiply their composite ratings during play selection

• Depth Matters: While starters are weighted heavily, backup contributions are included in most composites

• Position-Specific: Each position uses a mix of overall rating and specific composite rating (averaged)

• Game Settings: Many probabilities can be tuned via factor settings (passFactor, sackFactor, etc.)
